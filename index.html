02 Why PIZA
Creators are media companies with built-in distribution. The question is whether they’ll own what they build.
03 Manifesto

PIZA exists to shift power.

Culture has always been built by underrepresented creators, yet ownership has remained elsewhere.

PIZA closes the gap between influence and equity, replacing extractive systems with infrastructure designed for creators to lead, build, and own.

It turns attention into assets, and talent into long-term legacy.

07 Founder

Stephanie Piza

Stephanie Piza founded PIZA after exiting M88, where she served as Head of Emerging & Interactive Talent. She began her career in the digital talent and brand-partnerships divisions at CAA, then co-founded UNCMMN, one of the first female-founded management firms centered on culturally influential digital voices, alongside Charles D. King and Macro, before it was acquired and folded into M88.

Named to Variety’s “New Leaders” list, she has spent her career at the intersection of talent, brand, and digital, helping creators transform themselves into scalable businesses.
